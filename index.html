Something not working?

The site may be using an old saved copy of its files. Try a hard refresh first: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows, Linux) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac). If that does not help, clear this site's data:

  • Chrome, Edge: click the icon left of the address bar, open Site settings, then Delete data (Clear data in Edge).
  • Firefox: click the padlock, then Clear cookies and site data.
  • Safari: Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data, find this site and Remove.

Clearing site data also resets your saved scores and theme.
